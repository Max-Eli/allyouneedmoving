ABOUT US

Started with one box truck in Hollywood, Florida.

In 2012, Marco Delgado bought a used 16-footer and started moving apartments around Broward on weekends. He kept two rules: quote it honestly, and wrap everything like it's going to get dropped. Fourteen years later that's still the whole operating manual.

Today we run eleven trucks out of two yards, staff every job with W-2 employees — never day labor — and hold interstate authority to move you anywhere in the lower 48.
