## Important

**These are stock photographs, not photographs of this company.** They do not show
this company's crews, trucks, warehouse, or premises, and the alt text in
`src/content/images.ts` is written so that it never implies otherwise.

`area-palm-street.jpg` is a generic South Florida street used on both the Boca
Raton and Weston pages; its alt text is deliberately non-specific.

Replace these with real company photography before launch. Once you do, the alt
text can become specific ("our crew loading a 26-foot truck in Fort Lauderdale"),
which is better for both accessibility and local SEO.
